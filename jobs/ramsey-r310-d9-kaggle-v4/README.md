# D9-N9 specialized Kaggle CPU closure plan

Target: exclude a 9-regular triangle-free simple graph G of order 40 with independence number <=9, or find a valid witness. This is one of the 21 original closure obligations. It is not the whole R(3,10) question.

## Five exhaustive branches

For a vertex v, all 9 neighbors are independent. Each has 8 other neighbors, all among the 30 vertices nonadjacent to v. Their total codegree with v is 72, so the global maximum nonedge codegree c>=ceil(72/30)=3. For nonadjacent u,v with codegree c, the 20+c vertices T adjacent to neither (excluding u,v) induce a triangle-free graph of alpha<=7, since an independent 8-set there plus u,v is an I10. R(3,8)=28 implies c<=7. Therefore c=3,4,5,6,7 exactly exhaust D9. Choose a pair attaining c and label as 0,1. S=shared neighbors (c), X,Y=exclusive neighbor groups (9-c each), T=common nonneighbors (20+c). S,X and S,Y each independent. S vertices attach to T with degree 7 after root edges; X,Y vertices attach to T and across X,Y with remaining degree 8. Counting 180 edges gives e(T)=18+9c+e(X,Y). Published exact e(3,8,n) for n=23..27 are 49,56,65,73,85 (Goedgebeur/Radziszowski 2013 Table 3). Thus e(X,Y)>=4,2,2,1,4 respectively, and <=(9-c)^2. T has minimum internal degree >=c-3 by R(3,7)=23 and maximum <=7 (its neighborhood is independent). For c=3..6 partition the possible e(X,Y) interval into four disjoint contiguous intervals. For c=7, e(X,Y)=4 forces K2,2 between X and Y and e(T)=85. Published census contains exactly four isomorphism classes of (3,8;27,85). Fetch McKay's complete 477142 graph6 census, verify count, extract four edge-minimal graphs and verify each alpha<=7 and triangle-free. Four fixed core jobs exhaust c7 conditional on published census completeness. If the census fetch or checks fail, fall back to the exhaustive four values 4..7 of the first labeled T vertex's degree in T. No fixed-core restriction in fallback.

## CNF strengthening and soundness

Fix roots and all incident edges, force triangles forbidden, exact degree 9, global maximum codegree <=c, pair 0,1 attaining c, and maximality. A nonedge with no common neighbor gives an I10 (one endpoint plus nine neighbors of the other). Lex symmetry breaking: select the lexicographically largest row-major adjacency matrix among permutations preserving each of S,X,Y,T; swapping consecutive vertices in one group shows their adjacency vectors to all other vertices must be lex nonincreasing. In the fixed-core variant do not permute or sort T. For a root's nine independent neighbors A and subset B of size b, put H_B={nonneighbors of root with N(v) intersect A contained in B}. B union H_B belongs to common nonneighbors of the independent set A\B, so its alpha<=b. Hence |H_B|<=R(3,b+1)-1-b, i.e. [0,1,3,5,9,12,16,20] for b=0..7. For b=2 no three of H_B are independent; encode explicit clauses. All these are necessary, not unproved heuristic restrictions. PySAT sequential cardinality; custom conjunction and lex comparator truth tables checked. Separation targets independent 8-sets in T, independent 9-sets in both root duals, then general independent 10-sets. Every generated cut is a true original I10 exclusion clause; no heuristic blocking. A SAT candidate must pass independent full original graph verifier. An UNSAT relaxation is sufficient only after CNF audit and independent standalone Kissat DRAT replay via drat-trim. Store result, cuts, proof hashes and proof archive; timeout, cutoff, failure, or missing checker stays OPEN. Child UNSAT certificates cover parent only with all children solved and encoding/coverage audited.
