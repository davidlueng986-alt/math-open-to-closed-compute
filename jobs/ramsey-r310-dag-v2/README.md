# Ramsey R(3,10) executable closure DAG v2
W = a triangle-free simple 40-vertex graph with alpha<=9. A checked W implies R=41; excluding every W implies R=40 (published upper bound and verified Exoo lower bound).

## Cover theorem
Any W extends by adding triangle-free edges to a maximal triangle-free W. Its degrees are 5..9 (R(3,9)=36 and independently replayed degree-four exclusion, importing the published uniqueness of C35). For each nonedge u,v their common nonneighbors have alpha<=7 and order<=27 by R(3,8)=28, so deg(u)+deg(v)-|N(u) intersect N(v)|>=11. Saturation implies a common neighbor, hence sum>=12. Degree-5 vertices form a clique of size<=2; every degree-5 vertex must be adjacent to every degree-6 vertex. Two degree-5 vertices forbid degree-6 vertices; one has at most five degree-6 neighbors.

Set delta(v)=9-deg(v), D=sum delta, eps(v)=sum of neighbor deficits, m=180-D/2. H=G-(v union N(v)) has alpha<=8 and e(H)=180-D/2-9deg(v)+eps(v). For D>0 blow up each vertex to delta(v) independent twins and connect twin classes iff the originals are adjacent; the resulting triangle-free D-vertex graph has a vertex of degree <=D/2 by Mantel. Thus a root v with positive deficit has d=5..8 and e(H)<=180-9d. If D=0 all degrees are 9 and e(H)=99. (Adapted from Angeltveit Prop 2.1.) Relabel the root 0, its neighbors 1..d, and a minimum-degree neighbor as 1 with degree q. For d=5..8,q=5..9, and d=q=9, the 21 cases cover all possible saturated W, including asymmetric graphs. The root need not have global minimum degree. Every H has order 39-d, minimum internal degree>=11-d by R(3,8), and edge lower bound E[d]=129,118,104,95,86 respectively (published exact e(3,9,39-d)). Each H vertex has a neighbor in N(0) by saturation.

## Profile theorem
For degree counts n_i, L_i=max(0,E[i]-180+D/2+9i) <= eps(v) for every v of degree i. Bound eps by the i largest deficits of other vertices. The sum of eps equals sum n_i*i*(9-i); the deficit-weighted sum is <=D^2/2 by Mantel on the same blow-up. Enumerate all compositions of 40 into 5 counts, test parity, low-degree clique, and inequalities. The result is necessary, not sufficient. Profile enumeration independently gives 1578 survivors of 135751 and max deficit 42 (m>=159 for saturated W). No literature novelty claim.

## CORE-C35 auxiliary
A 35-vertex induced C35 core is 8-regular, so each core vertex has at most one outside neighbor. Any independent triple outside would have pairwise compatible independent neighborhoods in C35: each pair must hit every independent 8-set. Enlarge each to a maximal independent set; the checked compatibility graph has no triangles and no self-compatible row. Thus the five outside vertices induce alpha<=2 and triangle-free. Every degree is <=2 (independent neighborhoods) and >=2 (nonneighbors form a clique of size<=2); the graph is C5. For this fixed labeled cycle, each outside neighborhood is independent of size<=7, each core vertex is assigned at most once, and each nonadjacent outside pair hits all core independent 8-sets. These are necessary and sufficient for W containing C35; an exact SAT instance encodes them. This branch is auxiliary, not exhaustive for the original problem.

## Leaf closure
21 global branches: root degree d, min neighbor degree q. SAT base encodes the necessary bounds above, triangle-freeness and saturation. Refinement adds only genuine independent-10-set clauses, so unsatisfiability of any relaxation suffices to exclude its branch. A standalone rebuilt CNF plus successful DRAT replay required, including encoding audit; solver timeout/open certificate is not closure. SAT model checked against original target by independent verifier. The 21 exclusions are AND; a valid witness bypasses all exclusions (OR). PROFILE and CORE-C35 are useful auxiliary results only. Jobs bounded, not scheduled.
