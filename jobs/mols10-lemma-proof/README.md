# E074 odd-order four-layer rigidity obstruction

Let four OA(25,5,5,2) layers be coarsened rows and assign a bit to each symbol occurrence. A coarse pair bucket has one row per layer. Fine orthogonality means the two bits run over all four binary pairs, so each direction induces one of three nonzero partition colors in F2^2, namely (b0+b1,b0+b2). The two colors are unequal.

If three directions are fully rigid (the color for each coarse fibre is independent of the other direction and its symbol), their nonempty color sets are pairwise disjoint. There are only three colors, so each has one constant color and all three are different. Label them (1,0),(0,1),(1,1).

Choose any fibre of a fourth direction. Each of the three rigid directions gives five buckets, reindexing the same five rows in every layer. Hence the vector sum of the five colors is independent of which direction is used. Avoiding (1,0) makes the second component of every color 1. Avoiding (0,1) makes the first component 1. Since five is odd, the common vector sum is (1,1). But avoiding (1,1) forces the component sum of each color to be 1, and hence that of the common sum to be 1, contradiction.

The Lean source formalizes the bit-level orthogonality implication, odd-five parity contradiction, permutation invariance and the resulting forbidden three-color fibre condition. It does not formalize the complete reduction from arbitrary OA10 to four layers; no such reduction is known. The original problem remains OPEN. No novelty claim until literature review.
## Two additional analytic exclusions for aligned affine cosets

Let C={(x,y,x+y,x+2y,x+3y)} in F5^5 and Q=F5^5/C. Fix direction i and another direction t used to index a fibre. For each j != i and coset offset d in Q, let s_j(d) be the t-coordinate of the unique row in C+d whose i,j coordinates are zero. These are linear functions of d; s_t(d)=0. The map d -> (s_j(d)) for j != i,t is an isomorphism Q -> F5^3: if all shifts vanish, the row with i=t=0 in C+d also has every j=0 and hence d=0.

Balance of fine bits in every coarse pair bucket gives equations summing four cyclic translations of length-five binary functions to the constant 2. Fourier transform at every nontrivial fifth root produces a 4x4 matrix (zeta^{s_j(d_l)}), up to a common row reindex and choice of sign. Translate offsets so d_0=0. Subtract the all-one first row and column to reduce determinant to a 3x3 matrix zeta^{e_ij}-1. In the cyclotomic integer ring Z[zeta], (zeta^e-1)/(zeta-1) reduces to e under the quotient map zeta -> 1 mod 5. If the four offsets are affinely independent in Q, the exponent determinant is nonzero in F5, so the complex determinant is nonzero. Every nonconstant Fourier mode vanishes in every fibre; all directions are rigid. Thus affine independence is an exact analytic NO certificate. Lean formalizes the abstract arithmetic determinant step, not the entire cyclotomic instantiation or OA reduction.

For exactly one repeated coset, write layers C,C,D,E (after translation). The balance equations in each fibre involve three functions: the integer sum of the two C bits, the D bit, the E bit. Nonconstant Fourier modes are governed by 4x3 matrix with columns 1,zeta^{s_j(D)},zeta^{s_j(E)}. These columns are pairwise nonproportional, since equality of all relative shifts implies equality of cosets. If rank is below three, there is a linear relation with three nonzero complex coefficients. The pairs (zeta^s,zeta^r) then lie on a line a+bX+cY=0 with |X|=|Y|=1. Two distinct circles have at most two intersection points, hence there are at most two different shift pairs among the four rows.

If any row of D or E agrees with a C row in at least four coarse coordinates, the previously proved duplicate-four-agreement lemma already excludes the lift. Otherwise a shift value for D cannot occur three times (that would give four agreements). A rank-deficient direction therefore forces two disjoint repeated-shift pairs, giving two three-coordinate agreement triples of D with C, whose intersection is the chosen direction. For nonzero offset D, distinct agreement triples cannot share two coordinates (uniqueness on two coordinates would give at least four agreements), and three triples with pairwise intersections of size at most one cannot fit in five coordinates. Thus at most one direction has deficient rank. At least four directions have all three functions fibre-constant. In at most one of those directions can the two C copies agree in any fibre: two such fibres in different directions meet at a C row, contradicting strength two. In at least three directions the C copies therefore have complementary bits everywhere, and D,E have constant complementary bits per fibre. Compare the first C copy at any row with the D bit prescribed by the same coarse symbols. For every pair of these three directions their two comparison bits must differ, since the complementary pairs from C and from D/E have to be disjoint. Three pairwise distinct binary bits are impossible. This is an analytic proof of the one-repeated-coset exclusion, independent of the finite census.

The unrestricted question remains OPEN: four distinct coplanar offsets, arbitrary nonaligned stacks and OAs without a four-layer decomposition are not ruled out by these new analytic arguments.

## Stronger duplicate lemma, applicable to arbitrary four OA5 layers

Suppose two layers are copies of the same coarse OA C, and let D be one of the other two layers. Define d_i(p) as the XOR of the two copies' fine bits in direction i at p in C. If d_i is constant on every coarse fibre in four directions, at most one of these directions has any zero fibre: two zero fibres in distinct directions intersect at a point of C, and the duplicate fine rows would agree twice. Hence at least three directions are complementary everywhere.

In any pair i,j among these three, the D row matching a C row's coarse (i,j) pair is uniquely determined; this matching is a bijection. The two C rows have complementary fine pairs, and the D row has neither fine pair. Consequently the sum in F2 of the two fine bits on the first C copy and those on D equals 1. Sum this equality over the 25 C rows. The bijection makes the D sums independent of j. Writing S_i for the sum of the first C copy's and D's bits in direction i gives S_i+S_j=1 for every pair of three directions. The three equations are inconsistent. This strengthens the previous one-duplicate proof: no constancy assumption on the D/E bits is needed. Duplicate.lean formalizes the three-complementary-directions argument; audit status is separate from the v2 Proof.lean audit.

To apply the lemma to the actual E073 unresolved stacks, each fibre with C,C,D,E has 15 collapsed binary variables (5 each for duplicate XOR, D bit and E bit). Every pair bucket gives a parity equation d_C+b_D+b_E=0. Row-space inclusion of e_0+e_t for t=1,...,4 certifies constancy of d_C. For the last exceptional case use exact balance, testing 32 possible zero masks per fibre with a signed equality graph: for a complementary C pair, D and E differ; for equal C bits, D and E agree and their common bit is opposite the C bit, independent of the other coordinate. Each contradictory graph excludes its mask. In C,C,D,D cases rank nine in the collapsed ten-variable parity matrix implies constant duplicate XOR. All input layers are independently checked to be strength-two OAs of order five.

## Additional aligned-triple parity lemma

Suppose three directions' coarse functions coincide across the four layers after reindexing by a common base OA. At each of the 25 base points, the three partition colors are nonzero and pairwise distinct, hence sum to zero in F2^2. If two other directions are fully rigid, their color sets are disjoint and nonempty, so at least one direction uses a single constant color c. Take the nonzero linear functional on F2^2 with kernel {0,c}. For each of the aligned three directions, every bucket with that constant direction avoids c, so the functional of its color equals 1. Summing over all 25 buckets gives 1. The total color sum is independent of reindexing the same points in each layer. Summing the three functional totals therefore gives 1, contradicting the pointwise zero sum. Four aligned directions already contradict the existence of only three partition colors.
