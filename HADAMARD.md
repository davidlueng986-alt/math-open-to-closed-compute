# Hadamard conjecture research log

**Order 2396 is open in this project. The universal conjecture remains open.** The conjecture asks for a square sign matrix $H$ satisfying $HH^T=4tI$ for every positive integer $t$. Tensoring any solution with the order-two Hadamard matrix doubles its order, so a construction for every odd $t$ would suffice. The first gap in the pinned frontier tables is $4\cdot599=2396$. A matrix at that order would settle that instance. A proof of the conjecture must cover arbitrarily large odd $t$, or produce a general existence theorem with a verified finite remainder. [Prize statement](https://github.com/TheJustinSunPrize/awards/blob/10e0ab6e7503b6db769029d7d68cc4ca42c011d7/problems/catalog-0001-0100.md#JSP-000013) · [November frontier snapshot](https://github.com/vatskishan03/Hadamard/blob/d885022144ba6bf4efa8315e14871a3736947527/FRONTIER.md)

**Full-conjecture proof map.** A 14-step decomposition reduces the outstanding work to two uniform existence statements about incidence designs and exact binary rounding. Every admissible prefix has a kernel-checked rational next column. An order-20 fractional vertex shows where integrality enters. Published results guarantee roughly half the columns asymptotically and complete the last seven. A separate explicit construction at order 2396 supplies 1,196 pairwise orthogonal sign columns, checked against all 714,610 pairs. [Proof obligations, sources and witnesses](jobs/hadamard-all-orders/README.md) · [Lean rounding theorem](jobs/hadamard-all-orders/Rounding.lean)

**Verified rounding obstruction and repair.** A concrete order-28 residual design has exactly five eligible inside columns. They form a 20-column prefix with an exact rational next column and a parity obstruction to any binary extension. Its rank-eight defect lattice is $E_8$. Exhaustive integer checks rule out repairs that delete at most two columns. Deleting three gives an explicit full square, checked in Lean. For arbitrary odd $t$, the proof map now gives a full-width solution of the weight-mod-four and intersection-mod-two constraints for every outer design. Constructing the designs and lifting those congruences to exact integer counts remain open. [Parity and repair certificates](jobs/hadamard-all-orders/BlockedPrefix.lean) · [Exact checks and the congruence argument](jobs/hadamard-all-orders/README.md)

**Order-2396 stack restriction.** In the released alignment, any assembly of the outer design can retain at most 683 of the 1,196 sign columns. Even independently rearranging and signing the same source columns before stacking cannot make that entire stack extendible to the required outer design by three additions. [Integer row checks and scope](jobs/hadamard-all-orders/README.md#reproduction-and-proof-boundary)

**A second all-order route.** Directly constructing a near-square partial matrix bypasses the incidence design. At order 2396, any partial matrix with exactly 2,385 orthogonal sign columns has either 11 or 3 possible added columns up to sign, all mutually orthogonal. In the latter case the remaining defect is an integral $E_8$ lattice. This terminal classification is a written proof using public lattice classification, with the numerical bounds checked in Lean. For any such $E_8$ defect at this order, a determinant bound rules out repairs changing three or fewer old columns. The initial near-square construction and the $E_8$ escape remain open. [Terminal proof](jobs/hadamard-all-orders/TERMINAL_REPAIR.md)

**Direct-completion criterion for all odd multipliers.** The maximum number of mutually orthogonal appended columns equals the maximum number of unit lines in an integral unimodular overlattice of the specified residual lattice, when its parity index is four. Consequently mixed-parity partials of defect eight always complete, and defects nine through eleven always complete or extend to defect eight. At 2396, defect twelve is necessarily mixed and the exact criterion has at most two candidate lattices. Releasing up to eight columns of an integral $E_8$ defect leaves at most one possible new neighbor; a width gain occurs exactly when that neighbor exists and is cubic. Elkies's theorem gives an exact affine shortest-vector test for whether a candidate lattice is cubic. The determinant-one $D_{12}^{+}$ branch admits both Gram identities modulo eight, including at order 2396. Exact sign orthogonality is open in this model. [Defect-12 modular model](jobs/hadamard-all-orders/MOD8_DEFECT12.md) [Overlattice proof and order-28 replay](jobs/hadamard-all-orders/OVERLATTICES.md)

**Exterior stack threshold.** For odd $t\ge65$, a normalized $H(2t-2)/H(2t+2)$ stack needs at least five new columns to meet the exterior design equations. At 2396, the first five-column row profile meets all margins and new/new products but cannot meet the old/new products, by the inertia of its target Gram matrix. An alternative upper profile makes that Gram matrix positive definite but fails an integer Gram condition modulo 13. Separately, cycle structure and exact determinant residues exclude the entire $(1,299,300)$ intersection profile. The only remaining three-intersection profiles are $(286,299,303)$ and $(286,299,309)$. Both exclude point involutions with zero or two fixed points. ## Symmetries and the remaining search

For **either abelian group of order 1197**, a common automorphism group of a Legendre pair has order 1, 3 or 9. The determinant obstruction rules out even actions on order-3, -7 and -19 characters. A pure shear on the elementary-3 factor would force combined correlation at least 266 at one shift. The 399-point census excludes a simultaneous independent square action of order 27 on the remaining quotient. The full argument uses a written reduction and a finite enumeration. The shear, the involution calculation and two sparse-correlation lemmas have Lean proofs. ## Finite search boundary

All 8,521 surviving sparse orientations on the cyclic group received a binary lift search. Its best fixed-support full energy was 41,088. Its best 63-point quotient had error 7,424 counting all 63 shifts. Holding that endpoint's signed support fixed, exhaustive matching excludes an exact 63-point completion after up to three exchanges of positive and negative singleton signs per word. The neighborhood contained 100,897,593,729,541 possible pairs. Larger singleton changes and different supports are still available.
