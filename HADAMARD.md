# Hadamard conjecture research log

**Order 2396 is open in this project. The universal conjecture remains open.** The conjecture asks for a square sign matrix $H$ satisfying $HH^T=4tI$ for every positive integer $t$. Tensoring any solution with the order-two Hadamard matrix doubles its order, so a construction for every odd $t$ would suffice. The first gap in the pinned frontier tables is $4\cdot599=2396$. A matrix at that order would settle that instance. A proof of the conjecture must cover arbitrarily large odd $t$, or produce a general existence theorem with a verified finite remainder. ## Order 2396

An abelian Legendre pair would give a matrix of the required order from two normalized sign functions on a group of order 1197. For each of the 598 nonzero shift pairs up to reflection let $e(s)=P_A(s)+P_B(s)+2$. The full energy is $E=\sum_s e(s)^2$. A solution via this route requires $E=0$. ## Symmetries and the remaining search

For **either abelian group of order 1197**, a common automorphism group of a Legendre pair has order 1, 3 or 9. The determinant obstruction rules out even actions on order-3, -7 and -19 characters. A pure shear on the elementary-3 factor would force combined correlation at least 266 at one shift. The 399-point census excludes a simultaneous independent square action of order 27 on the remaining quotient. The full argument uses a written reduction and a finite enumeration. The shear, the involution calculation and two sparse-correlation lemmas have Lean proofs. [Proof and scope](jobs/hadamard-2396-399/symmetry/README.md) · [Lean theorems](jobs/hadamard-2396-399/Obstructions.lean)

The 18-shard run searched all 18 order-nine and 12 order-three subgroup representatives, plus the trivial subgroup, on each group law. There were 792 annealing and polishing schedules. Both lowest full energies came from the independent quadratic residues acting on the 19-coordinate. Under this symmetry, on the cyclic group, the exact length-63 norm forces **seven heavy positions** across the two words. Their combined sparse autocorrelation is 0 or −1 away from zero. An exhaustive support-and-sign enumeration followed by necessary quotient tests leaves 8,521 orientations of 3,763 templates. A surviving template can still fail the original sign equations.
